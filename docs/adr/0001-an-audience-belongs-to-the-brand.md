# An audience belongs to the brand

A brand holds the audiences it makes work for: who they are, what they want, what stands in the way, and which one is primary. A campaign references one of those rows instead of carrying its own free text. The ICP is a brand-level, slow-moving fact, so two campaigns on one brand cannot drift into two descriptions of the same people; a campaign still picks the slice it speaks to.

Considered and rejected: free text on both models (the state before), and a separate ICP/persona layer per campaign (the brand already knows its people; a second vocabulary would only get retyped).
